# Third-party assets

## Kenney Toon Characters

Character pose artwork is sourced from the Kenney asset library mirror:

- Source: https://github.com/shorepine/kenney
- Pack: `2d/Toon Characters`
- Variants used: Female person, Male person, Female adventurer, Male adventurer
- Frames used: selected HD walk frames
- License: CC0 1.0 / public domain dedication, as documented by the source repository

The game overlays its own Mid-Autumn star lantern artwork and visual effects on top of these character sprites.
